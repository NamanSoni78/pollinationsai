SciExplorer

Type any science topic and get an interactive visual explanation — a live, animated figure with sliders and toggles that actually change the mechanism, plus a cited walkthrough. In the spirit of Anima: show the real structure, cite every number, say how sure we are, make it move for a reason, educational never advice. Runs on your own Pollen.

Pick a topic and press Explore

SciExplorer turns a topic into a plan (what matters, in what order, with sources), a live figure (canvas or SVG, animated, with controls that change the physics — not decoration), and a cited walkthrough that says what was simplified.

Connect your Pollen (top right), then try a preset like Neuron firing or Why the sky is blue. You pay only for what you generate, from your own balance.
